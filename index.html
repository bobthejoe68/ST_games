about us

hello! shrimptooth games is a miniscule indie studio founded by josh edwards (that's me). our mission is to bring together artists, composers, voice actors, and generally cool people to craft some pretty fun games.

i first started making games when i was 10 years old, smashing together blocks of code to make animations, small games, and eventually some larger games too.

now, almost 20 years on (ouch), i still can't wait to start on new projects, collaborate with rad people, and create stories that surprise, resonate, or just make people laugh.

it takes a village (or should i say an aquarium) to make a game, and this passion is at the heart of shrimptooth games. i hope you enjoy the weird little things we create.
